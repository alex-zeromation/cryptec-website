Custom Software Solutions

Software written for your systems, not around them.

From embedded firmware to integration services and fleet dashboards, we take a points list, a datasheet or a rough brief and deliver software that runs on it — documented, updatable and yours.

01

Embedded firmware & device images

Firmware for controllers, gateways and OEM hardware, or a locked-down device image for a panel, kiosk or sensor. Boot to your application, nothing else — no launcher, no store, no stray settings for anyone to find.

·Locked-down images with watchdog and auto-recovery
·Signed over-the-air updates, staged per site or per device
·Certificate-based device identity provisioned at build time
·Rollback to the last known-good image on a failed update
02

Integration software & protocol drivers

Services that sit between systems and translate — building management to dashboard, meter to billing, intercom to phone. Where a system has an open protocol we speak it natively; where it doesn't, we write the driver against the vendor's documentation.

BACnet/IP BACnet MS/TP Modbus TCP/IP Modbus RTU SIP video intercom MQTT REST & webhooks Weather APIs
03

Fleet monitoring & management tooling

A dashboard for the people who own the estate: what's online, what firmware it runs, which integration went quiet and when. Devices self-check every connection they depend on and report back, so a failed gateway is known before someone calls it in.

·Per-device telemetry, alerting and escalation to your team
·Firmware and application version inventory across sites
·Energy and usage reporting exported for billing or ESG
·Monthly reporting, with an engineer on the escalation

How an engagement runs.

Scope

We read the points list, the drawings and whatever documentation the incumbent systems have, then write down exactly what the software will do.

Build

Development against real hardware on the bench, reviewed with you as it lands rather than at the end.

Commission

On site, point by point, until every integration reads and writes what it should. Handover comes documented.

Support

Updates and changes across the life of the fleet. The software is yours; we stay on for as long as it's useful that we do.
